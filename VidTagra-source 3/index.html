Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about inspecting YouTube tags.

Can I inspect tags from any YouTube video?−
Tags are available only when a public video includes uploader-added tags. Private, removed, or restricted videos may not return tag data.
What does the relevance score mean?+
It estimates how closely a tag matches the video’s title and description. It is not search volume or a prediction of ranking performance.
Should I copy another creator’s tags?+
Use extracted tags for research. Remove brand names and unrelated phrases, and keep only terms that accurately describe your video.
Why did my search return no tags?+
Some creators do not add tags. Check that the link is correct and that the video is publicly available.
